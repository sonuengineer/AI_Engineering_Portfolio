# Sales Playbook -- Handling Declining Accounts

## Detection
An account is considered declining when completed-order revenue over the most recent six months is at least 10 percent lower than the previous six months.

## Investigation checklist
Review ERP order history and delivery performance. Review recent customer emails and meeting notes. Check open quotations and contract status. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions before contacting the customer.

## Customer outreach
Outreach emails must be reviewed by a sales manager before sending. Do not disclose internal revenue analysis to customers. Do not offer discounts above 5 percent without pricing-desk approval.
