# Returns and Quality Policy

## Non-conformance
When a customer rejects a batch, the quality team opens a non-conformance report (NCR) within 2 business days and provides a root-cause analysis within 15 business days.

## Credits
Customers receive a full credit for rejected batches confirmed as non-conforming. Replacement shipments are prioritized and dispatched within 5 business days.

## Escalation
Repeated non-conformance for the same customer within 90 days must be escalated to the head of quality.
