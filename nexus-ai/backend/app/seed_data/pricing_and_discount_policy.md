# Pricing and Discount Policy

## Discount authority
Account managers may approve discounts of up to 5 percent. Discounts between 5 and 12 percent require approval from the pricing desk. Discounts above 12 percent require approval from the Chief Commercial Officer.

## Volume tiers
Volume-based pricing tiers are available for annual commitments above 250,000 USD. Tier proposals must include a margin impact estimate.

## Competitive matching
Competitive price matching requires written evidence of the competitor offer and is limited to products in the standard catalog.
