# Summit Foods Processing -- Call Notes (July 2026)

## Context
Summit Foods Processing told Acme that its parent company announced a capital expenditure budget freeze for the second half of 2026.
All non-critical maintenance, repair and operations purchases above 5,000 USD now need parent-company approval.

## Impact
Summit expects to defer conveyor spare-part orders until the budget freeze is lifted, likely in January 2027.
Summit emphasized that the relationship with Acme is not at risk and that service quality has been satisfactory.
